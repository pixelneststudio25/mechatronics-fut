Welcome to Mechatronics Engineering

The Department of Mechatronics Engineering at the Federal University of Technology, Minna, is a centre of excellence in the interdisciplinary field of mechanical, electronics, computer and control engineering. Our mission is to produce graduates with the skills to design and create intelligent systems and products that are smart and efficient.

Established in 2010, we have grown to become one of the most sought-after engineering departments in Nigeria, with state-of-the-art laboratories and a dedicated faculty.

The field

What is mechatronics?

Mechatronics brings four engineering disciplines together to build intelligent systems, from smart machines to automated products.

Mechanical

Machines, motion and structures.

Electronics

Circuits, sensors and power.

Computer

Software and embedded systems.

Control

Making systems respond and stay stable.

Vision and mission

Our vision

To be a leading department in mechatronics education and research in Nigeria, fostering innovation and entrepreneurship.

Our mission

To provide a conducive learning environment, engage in cutting-edge research, and produce graduates who can solve real-world problems through mechatronics.

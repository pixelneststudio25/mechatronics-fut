What this website collects, why, and what we do with it.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to the Department of Mechatronics Engineering, Federal University of Technology, Minna. It is maintained by the department and NAMTES FUT Minna.

What we collect, and why

When you send us a message

The Contact page and the Partner with us page collect the details you type into them: your name, email address, organisation (partner form), your role and topic, and your message. We use these only to read and reply to your enquiry.

These messages are delivered to the department's email inbox through a form service called Web3Forms, which handles them on our behalf.

When you browse the site

  • We do not use advertising, tracking cookies, or analytics tools.
  • Our hosting provider, Vercel, and our database provider, Supabase, receive standard technical information such as your IP address whenever your browser loads a page or fetches content. This is how websites work, and we do not use it to identify you.
  • Fonts and images are served from this site, not from Google Fonts or other outside services.

Information stored in your browser

To make the site faster and more reliable, your browser keeps a few small items. We do not use them to track you.

  • A saved copy of the latest news, events and resources, so they still show if your connection drops.
  • The time of your last message, so the forms can stop accidental repeat sends.
  • For admins only: a sign-in session on the admin pages.

Who sees your information

Only the people who read the department's inbox can see your messages. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing.

The services named above (Web3Forms, Vercel and Supabase) process data on our behalf. Links to other sites, such as Google Drive and our social media pages, are covered by those sites' own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep messages only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for ordinary record keeping.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We aim to handle personal data in line with Nigerian data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Changes to this page

If we change how the site handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
